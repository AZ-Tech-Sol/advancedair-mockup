Residential

Repair & 24-hour service

Complete repair and 24-hour service on any heating or air conditioning system. Even if we did not install your current system, we can service or repair it — and we honor the existing manufacturer warranty.

We send a repair associate rather than a sales representative, which is why problems are often addressed and sometimes solved the same day.

Installation & replacement

New R-410a high-efficiency systems, add-on replacements, and custom duct work fabrication. We install and service Amana, Trane, Rheem, American Standard, Tempstar, Ruud, Goodman, Lennox, Carrier and Honeywell, among others.

Heat-load calculation

We size your system against the heat that actually enters and is produced in your home, rather than guessing from square footage. This step is frequently overlooked and is a necessity in Arizona because of the summer temperatures.

Maintenance programs

A custom maintenance program designed around your system, to prevent untimely or costly mechanical failure. A trained maintenance associate will help you work out what your home actually needs.

Air quality

Air purification and high-efficiency systems, indoor air quality, dehumidification, humidification and ventilation. Free consultations and energy audits.

New home construction
