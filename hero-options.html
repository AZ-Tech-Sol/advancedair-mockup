The hero line: pick one

Option A is live on the mockup right now and it is mine, not theirs — the only invented piece of voice on the page, and the first thing Angela reads. The other three come from their own words. Same hero, same everything else; only the line changes.

A — live nowWritten by me. Weather as the urgency.

Tucson summers don’t wait. Neither do we.

Certified technicians, 24/7 emergency service, and repairs on every major system — whether we installed it or not.

📞 Call (520) 349-9606See this month’s coupons →
For: memorable, local, and it sells urgency without fear. Against: it is a slogan I invented for a business I have never spoken to, and it is summer-only — it reads oddly in January when the heating calls come in.
B — their emergency framingPlainest. Their own red-bar wording.

No heat? No cooling? We answer 24/7.

Certified technicians and repairs on every major system — whether we installed it or not. Serving Tucson and all surrounding areas.

📞 Call (520) 349-9606See this month’s coupons →
For: it asks the question the visitor is already asking, works all year, and invents nothing. Against: it is functional rather than distinctive, and it currently duplicates the red bar above it — one would have to change.
C — pure client canonTheir own tagline, promoted to the hero.

Your Local Energy Experts!

Heating and air conditioning repair, installation and maintenance across Tucson — 24 hours a day, licensed ROC-K-39 265686.

📞 Call (520) 349-9606See this month’s coupons →
For: it is literally theirs, it is what the site has said for years, and nobody has to approve a new voice. Against: “energy experts” does not say heating and cooling to someone who has never heard of them, and it says nothing about urgency.
D — service-firstSays the job and the timeframe.

Heating and air conditioning, repaired today.

Any brand, any installer, 24 hours a day — and we honor the existing manufacturer warranty. Serving Tucson and all surrounding areas.

📞 Call (520) 349-9606See this month’s coupons →
For: clearest to a stranger and to Google — it names the service and the speed. Against: “today” is a promise the business has to be able to keep, so it needs Dennis’s word, not ours.

Tell me a letter and it is on the live mockup in two minutes. If none of them land, D is the one to rewrite — it is the only shape that says what they do to somebody who has never heard of them.
